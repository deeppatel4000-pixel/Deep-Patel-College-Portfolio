ORBIX

An aerospace application for looking closely at real vehicles — aircraft and rocket profiles, side-by-side comparison, an engineering lab, mission control and replay, and a research section.

Type
Web application
Role
Design and build
Status
Deployed
Live
orbix-aerospace.vercel.app

What it does

You can browse aircraft and rockets, open a profile for a single vehicle, put two vehicles side by side, work through the engineering lab, follow a mission in mission control, replay one after the fact, and read through the research section.
