Projects

These are the two projects I have taken the furthest so far. ORBIX is an aerospace web app I kept expanding as I found new problems I wanted it to solve. The bell nozzle began as a propulsion design, then took me through CAD, CFD, debugging, and fabrication. The case studies show the work behind both.

01

Software · Aerospace web application · 2026

ORBIX

ORBIX currently covers 10 vehicles, five aircraft and five launch vehicles. Each value keeps its units, qualifier, and source. Users can compare vehicles, work through 33 Engineering Lab modules across six workflows, and use mission tools for preparation, analysis, simulation, and replay.

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · Vitest · Playwright

02

Engineering · Self-directed propulsion project · April 2026

Bell Nozzle

A bell nozzle drawn in OpenSCAD from a six-point profile, meshed and run through SimFlow and OpenFOAM, and printed in PLA. The simulation never converged, so most of the project became working out why, and the case study keeps that result rather than dressing it up.

OpenSCAD · SimFlow · OpenFOAM · ParaView · FDM
