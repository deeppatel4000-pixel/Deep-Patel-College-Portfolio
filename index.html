02 Engineering · Design and analysis

Optimized Bell Nozzle

A Mach 3.5 LOX/RP-1 nozzle sized from NASA CEA data for a 100 bar chamber and a 0.7 bar exit, drawn as a parametric shell and meshed for a compressible solver. The solve diverged around iteration 24. Working out why it diverged turned out to be the useful part of the project.

About
